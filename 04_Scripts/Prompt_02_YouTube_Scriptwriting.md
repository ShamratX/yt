# Prompt 02: YouTube Scriptwriting Prompt (`Human Mismatch`)

Run this AFTER verifying your research notes with `Prompt_01_Topic_Research.md`. Replace `[VIDEO_TOPIC]`, `[VIDEO_DURATION]`, and `[RESEARCH_NOTES]`.

---

```text
Act as the lead documentary scriptwriter for the YouTube channel "Human Mismatch" (tagline: "Ancient hardware. Modern world.").

VIDEO TOPIC: [VIDEO_TOPIC]
TARGET VIDEO DURATION: [VIDEO_DURATION] (default: 13–14 minutes ≈ 1,950–2,150 spoken words)

VERIFIED RESEARCH NOTES & SOURCES TO USE (do NOT invent fake studies or numbers):
[RESEARCH_NOTES]

WRITING STYLE & VOICE:
- Tone: Warm, intelligent, curious documentary storyteller—conversational spoken English, slightly witty where natural, never preachy or robotic.
- Sentence rhythm: Short, crisp sentences (10–18 words average) that sound effortless when read aloud.
- Zero AI clichés: NEVER use phrases like "In today's fast-paced world", "delve into", "tapestry", "testament", "it's important to note", "buckle up", or "let's dive in".
- Zero medical/diagnostic advice: Frame everything strictly as evolutionary history, anthropology, and published science.
- 100% original storytelling: Do NOT copy competitor scripts or phrasing.

MANDATORY SCRIPT STRUCTURE:
1. HOOK (0:00–0:45 | ~110 words):
   - Open directly in a vivid, relatable second-person modern moment (NO channel intro, NO "Welcome back!").
   - State the common modern assumption, flip it into a surprising Darwinian/historical paradox, and open 1 irresistible mystery loop backed by real evidence.
2. 6 TO 7 ESCALATING CHAPTERS (0:45–11:30):
   - Give each chapter a clear mini-question and smooth bridge transition.
   - Translate every scientific finding into a simple everyday analogy (ELI5 clarity).
   - Plant 3 subtle "Remember X" breadcrumbs early and pay all 3 off later in the script.
   - Include 1 natural Viewer Quiz moment around the halfway mark ("Quick guess before I tell you...").
   - Honestly acknowledge any real scientific debate without killing momentum.
3. MODERN PAYOFF (11:30–12:45):
   - Connect the ancient discovery directly back to the viewer's modern daily life with a memorable reframing insight.
4. FULL-CIRCLE OUTRO (12:45–13:30):
   - Echo the opening 15-second image poetically.
   - Do NOT beg "smash that like and subscribe button". Instead, end cleanly and bridge smoothly into one related human-mismatch curiosity question.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
- Clean chapter headers (`## CHAPTER 1: ...`)
- Full word-for-word narration ready to read aloud
- Estimated total word count at the bottom.
```
