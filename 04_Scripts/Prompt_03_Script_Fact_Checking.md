# Prompt 03: Script Fact-Checking & Anti-AI Polish

Run this AFTER drafting your script and BEFORE generating your voiceover. Replace `[VIDEO_TOPIC]` and `[SCRIPT]`.

---

```text
Act as a strict documentary fact-checker and senior YouTube script editor.

VIDEO TOPIC: [VIDEO_TOPIC]

Review the draft script below and perform FOUR checks:

1. FACT & SOURCE AUDIT:
   - Flag every statistic, date, fossil site, researcher name, or scientific claim that looks uncertain, exaggerated, or missing verification.
   - Check that the script does NOT give medical, psychiatric, legal, or investment advice (it must stay strictly educational/historical storytelling).
   - Check that nothing copies competitor videos or repeats misleading myths.

2. AI-CLICHÉ CLEANUP:
   - Find and remove robotic AI phrases (such as "In today's fast-paced world", "delve into", "tapestry", "testament to", "it's important to note", "let's dive in", or leftover stage cues like "pattern interrupt").

3. RETENTION & SPOKEN FLOW CHECK:
   - Is the first 30 seconds instantly engaging and clear?
   - Flag any paragraph that drags, repeats an earlier point, or sounds like a textbook instead of natural spoken English.
   - Suggest tighter, punchier rewrites for any weak transitions.

4. VERIFIED SOURCE LIST:
   - Produce a clean bulleted list of verified sources ready to paste into the YouTube description.

DRAFT SCRIPT:
[SCRIPT]
```
