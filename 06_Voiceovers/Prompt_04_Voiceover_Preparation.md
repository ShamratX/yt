# Prompt 04: Voiceover Preparation (Clean Spoken Script)

Run this AFTER your script is approved to prepare clean, natural text for your English TTS voiceover tool (Google Cloud TTS, Azure AI Speech, Kokoro TTS, Clipchamp, or ElevenLabs Paid).

---

```text
Prepare the approved YouTube script below for natural English AI voiceover generation.

RULES:
1. Do NOT change the meaning, facts, or storytelling structure of the approved script.
2. Remove all markdown symbols, chapter headings, visual cues, bracketed notes, URLs, and citation numbers so the voice never accidentally reads them out loud.
3. Convert hard-to-pronounce abbreviations, years, and scientific terms into clean spoken English (e.g., "3:07 AM" -> "three oh seven A M", "BCE" -> "B C E", "40,000" -> "forty thousand").
4. Break the text into clean 250-to-350-word chunks labeled CHUNK 1, CHUNK 2, etc., splitting ONLY at natural chapter or thought breaks so I can generate consistent audio cleanly.
5. Keep natural punctuation (commas, periods, short pauses) so the narrator breathes smoothly and sounds calm, curious, and documentary-grade.
6. Flag any unusual proper nouns or Latin/scientific words at the bottom with simple phonetic spelling so I can double-check pronunciation.

APPROVED SCRIPT:
[SCRIPT]
```
