# Prompt 01: Topic Research & Verified Source Finder

Copy and paste the prompt below into Claude or ChatGPT (with web search turned on). Replace `[VIDEO_TOPIC]` before sending.

---

```text
Act as an investigative science and anthropology researcher preparing factual notes for an educational YouTube documentary.

VIDEO TOPIC: [VIDEO_TOPIC]
TARGET VIDEO LENGTH: [VIDEO_DURATION] (default: 13–15 minutes)

Research this topic deeply and give me clear, accurate, easy-to-understand notes with these exact sections:

1. THE COMMON MYTH (What most people assume about this topic today).
2. THE SURPRISING REALITY (The real evolutionary, anthropological, or historical explanation that flips the myth).
3. 5 TO 6 REAL, VERIFIABLE SOURCES (Include exact researcher names, publication years, journal/institution names, and core findings from peer-reviewed papers, anthropology studies, Smithsonian, Nature, PubMed, or respected history books. Do NOT invent or guess citations—if you are unsure of a study year or author, say "UNVERIFIED").
4. 3 "WAIT, REALLY?!" CONCRETE FACTS (Specific numbers, fossils, ancient sites, or famous experiments that create instant curiosity).
5. MODERN-LIFE CONNECTION (How this ancient trait or history directly affects a normal person's modern daily life).
6. SCIENTIFIC DEBATES OR LIMITS (Note honestly where scientists still disagree so the video stays credible and accurate).
7. 1 FUN VIEWER QUIZ QUESTION (A surprising multiple-choice question with 3–4 answers and the correct answer explained, to place mid-video).

Keep language simple, clear, and free of academic jargon.
```
