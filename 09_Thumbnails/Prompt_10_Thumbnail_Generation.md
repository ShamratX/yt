# Prompt 10: Thumbnail Concept & Artwork Prompt (`Human Mismatch`)

Use this prompt to design **2 distinct A/B thumbnail concepts** (`thumb_A.png` and `thumb_B.png`) for YouTube's **Test & Compare** feature. Replace `[VIDEO_TOPIC]` and `[VIDEO_TITLE]`.

---

```text
Act as a senior YouTube packaging strategist and editorial illustrator for the channel "Human Mismatch".

VIDEO TOPIC: [VIDEO_TOPIC]
WORKING TITLE: [VIDEO_TITLE]

Create TWO genuinely different, high-CTR YouTube thumbnail concepts (1280x720, 16:9) so I can A/B test them in YouTube Studio:
- CONCEPT A: Split-Screen Modern vs. Ancient Contrast (visualizes "Ancient hardware. Modern world." in 1 second).
- CONCEPT B: Single Bold Expressive Close-Up / Curiosity Object (clean focal silhouette + instant emotional tension).

LOCKED CHANNEL VISUAL STYLE:
- **Minimal, clean, uncluttered flat 2D editorial illustration** with crisp charcoal outlines (#1E2228), smooth flat color fills, high contrast for tiny mobile screens, and **maximum 2–3 cohesive colors** (warm cream #F6F1E7 or midnight slate #25344A background + 1–2 bold focal accents like terracotta #C85A32 or amber #E59A3C).
- NO messy multi-color rainbow clutter, NO generic white-circle stickmen, NO busy backgrounds, NO more than 3 visual elements.

MANDATORY ORIGINAL CONTENT & VISUAL RULES:
- Create original visuals specifically for this video.
- Do not reuse the same scenes, images or animations from previous videos.
- Avoid repetitive AI-generated templates.
- Give every thumbnail a unique visual composition.
- Match visuals directly with the video's core mystery.
- Use clear framing, expressive faces, and high-contrast backgrounds.
- Avoid generic stock-looking visuals.
- Do not copy competitor thumbnails or artwork.
- Do not create misleading historical or scientific visuals.
- Keep a consistent overall channel art style while making each episode's thumbnail instantly distinctive.

TEXT OVERLAY RULE:
- Recommend 0 to 3 short bold words MAX per concept (NEVER repeat the full video title!). Tell me exact placement so I can add crisp text cleanly in Canva or Photopea (generate the AI art text-free!).

For BOTH Concept A and Concept B, provide:
1. Visual layout & why a cold viewer stops scrolling.
2. Exact text-free AI image prompt (16:9) ready to paste into Leonardo.ai / Ideogram / Midjourney.
3. Exact 0–3 word text overlay suggestion + color + placement for Canva/Photopea.
```
