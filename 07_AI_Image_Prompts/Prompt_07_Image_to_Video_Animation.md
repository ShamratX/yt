# Prompt 07: Optional Image-to-Video Subtle Animation Prompt

> **Note:** On our free \$0/mo workflow, you do **NOT** need AI video generators—smooth FFmpeg camera moves (`assemble_video.py`) already deliver high retention. Use this prompt ONLY if you later upgrade to a **paid commercial tier** of Runway, Kling, or Hailuo to gently animate 2–3 hero establishing shots per episode (never use watermarked free-tier video generators on YouTube).

---

```text
Write subtle, documentary-grade image-to-video motion prompts for 2 to 3 key hero scenes from [VIDEO_TOPIC].

SOURCE IMAGES: [SCENE_IDS_AND_DESCRIPTIONS]
CLIP DURATION: 4–5 seconds each

ANIMATION DIRECTION RULES:
- Create original visuals specifically for this video.
- Do not reuse the same scenes, images or animations from previous videos.
- Avoid repetitive AI-generated templates.
- Give every scene a unique visual composition and camera movement.
- Match visuals with the narration.
- Use different camera angles, framing, backgrounds and illustrations.
- Avoid generic stock-looking visuals.
- Do not copy competitor videos, scripts, thumbnails or artwork.
- Do not create misleading historical or scientific visuals.
- Keep a consistent overall channel art style (preserve crisp editorial ink-and-watercolor lines — NO morphing into 3D or photorealism).
- Avoid unnecessary repetitive animations and slideshow-style videos — keep motion calm, physical, and natural (e.g., gently drifting campfire embers, swaying savanna grass, softly blinking bedside clock light, gentle snowfall, slow camera push-in).
- Every video must have its own original storytelling and visual identity.

For each selected hero scene, provide a concise 1–2 sentence motion prompt plus motion-strength guidance (low/subtle) so the 2D art never warps.
```
