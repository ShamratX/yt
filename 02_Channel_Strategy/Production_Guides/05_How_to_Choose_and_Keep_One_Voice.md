# Step 5 Guide: Where to Get Suitable Voices & Keep ONE Consistent Voice

> **Why Consistency Matters:** Viewers build a subconscious bond with a channel's narrator. Changing voices every two videos makes a channel feel like a random AI content farm and hurts returning-viewer rates.

---

|---|---|---|
| **\$0/mo Best Cloud Voices** | **Google Cloud TTS** (`Chirp 3: HD` / `Neural2`) | Calm conversational American or British documentary narrator (e.g., `en-US-Chirp3-HD-*` male/female mid-tone) |

---

## 2. The 10-Minute "Voice Audition" Test (Do This Once Before Video #1)
1. Take the **first 45 seconds** of Video #1 (*3 AM Wakeup*).
2. Render that exact paragraph using **3 different candidate voices**.
3. Listen to all three on **your phone speaker** and on **headphones** while looking away from the screen.
4. Pick the voice that passes these 4 checks:
   - Sounds friendly and curious (not bored, not yelling).
   - Pronounces pauses, questions, and numbers naturally.
   - Easy to listen to for 15 straight minutes without ear fatigue.
   - Fits both serious science moments and light dry humor.

---

## 3. Lock Your "Channel Voice Card" Forever
Once chosen, write your exact locked settings into `06_Voiceovers/Voiceover_Guide_and_Licensing.md` and use the **exact same voice ID, speed, and export settings** on every future video:
- **Never switch voice models mid-video.**
- **Only upgrade voices once** (for example, moving from Google Cloud TTS Free to ElevenLabs Paid once you are ready to scale)—and keep the new voice locked from that point forward.
