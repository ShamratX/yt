# Step 6 Guide: How to Generate Original Images and Illustrations

> **Goal:** Create **55 to 75 original, cohesive editorial illustrations** per 13-minute video so every scene feels custom-crafted—never like a repetitive AI slideshow.

---

## 1. Our Signature Channel Art Style (Beating "Stickman Fatigue")
Competitors (`@Inkexplainer96`, `@Basically-Primitive`, `@Neon-Rush-3D`, `@AncestorFiless`) all use plain white-circle stickmen. To stand out immediately on YouTube Home feeds:
- **Style:** Warm **editorial ink-and-watercolor 2D illustration** (crisp charcoal ink outlines + soft flat/textured gouache washes on warm off-white/cream paper texture).
- **Color Palette:** Warm Cream/Parchment (`#F6F1E7`), Deep Charcoal Ink (`#1E2228`), Terracotta Rust (`#C85A32`), Forest Sage (`#4E6E58`), Ochre Gold (`#D9A441`), Midnight Slate (`#25344A` for night scenes).
- **Aspect Ratio:** Always **16:9 widescreen** (`1920×1080` or `1344×768` upscaled cleanly).

*(Full character rules are locked in `05_characters/Channel_Art_Style_and_Characters.md`.)*

---

## 2. Best Commercial-Safe Image Generators
- **Free (\$0/mo) Starter:** **Leonardo.ai Free** (`150 fast tokens/day` — commercial use allowed; generate ~15–20 scenes per day across 3–4 days) + **Flux.1 Schnell** (Apache 2.0 open weights) + **Canva Free** (for 6–8 crisp comparison charts/diagrams per video!).
- **Paid Upgrade (\$8–\$12/mo):** **Midjourney Basic (\$10/mo)** or **Leonardo Essential (\$12/mo)** — unlocks private generations and locked `--sref` (style reference) consistency.
- **NEVER USE:** Bing Image Creator (strictly non-commercial) or copyrighted competitor screenshots.

---

## 3. Mandatory Originality Rules (Apply to Every Single Video)
1. **Create 100% original visuals specifically for each episode**—never copy/paste the same scenes from Episode 1 into Episode 2.
2. **Change framing every single cut:** Alternate wide establishing shots, medium character reactions, close-up object details, overhead maps, split-screen Modern-vs-Ancient contrasts, and clean Canva science diagrams.
3. **Never put gibberish AI text inside generated art:** Keep AI illustrations text-free; add crisp labels/dates/numbers cleanly in Canva or our FFmpeg/editor step.
4. **Check scientific/historical accuracy:** No cavemen hunting dinosaurs, no medieval castles in the Ice Age, no extra fingers or distorted faces.

---

## 4. Simple File Naming Rule (Required for Auto-Assembly!)
Save every approved image inside your episode folder `04_images/` using strict 3-digit numbering matching your scene plan:
- `scene_001.png`
- `scene_002.png`
- `scene_003.png`
- ... up to `scene_065.png`
