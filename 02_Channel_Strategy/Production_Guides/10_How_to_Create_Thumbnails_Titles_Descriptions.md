# Step 10 Guide: How to Create Thumbnails, Titles and Descriptions

> **Reality Check:** On YouTube, **packaging (Topic + Title + Thumbnail) controls 80% of whether anyone ever watches your video.** Never rush thumbnails in the last 5 minutes before upload.

---

## 1. Thumbnail Formula (Anti-Stickman-Fatigue System)
1. **Canvas Size:** `1280 × 720 px` PNG/JPG (under 2 MB).
2. **Visual Style:** Match our warm editorial ink-and-watercolor art—NOT generic white-circle stickmen.
3. **One Instant Visual Story (3 Elements Max):**
   - *Best pattern:* **Split-Screen Modern vs. Ancient** (e.g., exhausted modern character staring at a glowing `3:00 AM` clock on the left vs. glowing predator eyes outside a prehistoric campfire on the right).
   - *Alternate pattern:* **Single Expressive Character Close-Up** showing unmistakable emotion (wide-eyed panic, disbelief, exhaustion) + 1 simple visual object.
4. **Text Rule:** **0 to 3 words MAX**, bold high-contrast typography, **never repeating the exact video title!**
   - *Title:* `Why Your Brain Wakes You Up at 3 AM (It Saved Your Ancestors)`
   - *Thumbnail text:* `3:00 AM` or `NOT INSOMNIA`
5. **The Mobile Squint Test:** Shrink your thumbnail down to the size of a postage stamp on screen. If you cannot instantly tell what the picture shows in 1 second, simplify it!
6. **Mandatory A/B Testing:** Always build **2 distinct concepts** (`thumb_A.png` & `thumb_B.png`) using `09_Thumbnails/Prompt_10_Thumbnail_Generation.md` + Canva Free / Photopea, and upload both into YouTube Studio's **Test & Compare**.

---

## 2. High-CTR Title Rules
- Keep length between **50 and 65 characters** so mobile screens don't cut off the twist.
- Use our 4 proven templates:
  1. `Why Your Brain [Relatable Annoyance] (It Saved Your Ancestors)`
  2. `Why Evolution Never Deleted [Seemingly Bad Trait]`
  3. `Why [Surprising Habit] Built Human Civilization (Not [Obvious Thing])`
  4. `How Ancient Humans Survived [Visceral Problem] Without [Modern Tool]`

---

## 3. Description Structure (Modeled on Top-Performing Research Channels)
Use `09_Thumbnails/Prompt_11_SEO_Title_and_Description.md` to generate a clean description with 4 parts:
1. **Lines 1–2 (Above the fold):** A crisp 2-sentence curiosity summary containing your primary search keywords naturally.
2. **Chapters (`00:00` ...):** Exact matching timestamps from your final video (always verify the last timestamp is *inside* the video's duration!).
3. **Research & Sources (`📚 RESEARCH & SOURCES`):** Bullet list of the 3–6 real academic studies/articles you verified in Step 2 (huge trust signal!).
4. **Interactive Bonus:** Add **1 YouTube Quiz** inside YouTube Studio (Editor/Video elements) around minute 5–6 to boost engagement and retention.
