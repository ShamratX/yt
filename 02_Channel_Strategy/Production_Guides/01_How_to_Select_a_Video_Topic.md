# Step 1 Guide: How to Select a Winning Video Topic

> **Goal:** Pick a topic that cold viewers (who have never heard of your channel) will instantly want to click—and that fits smoothly into our 4 Content Pillars.

---

## The Simple 4-Question Topic Filter
Before starting any video, pick a candidate from `03_Topic_Ideas/Topic_Database_100_Ideas.md` and check it against these 4 questions:

1. **The "Wait, Why?!" Test:** Does the topic make a normal person pause for 1 second and think *"Wait... I never realized that—why DOES that happen?"*
   - *Strong example:* **Why Your Brain Wakes You Up at 3 AM (It Saved Your Ancestors)**
   - *Weak example:* *A History of Human Sleep Patterns* (sounds like a boring school textbook)
2. **The Universal Relatability Test:** Do at least 50% of adults worldwide experience or recognize this?
   - Sleep, toothaches, blushing, nightmares, procrastination, music chills, back pain = **YES (global appeal)**.
3. **The Visual Variety Test:** Can you picture at least 10 different interesting scenes (modern life, ancient tribes, brain/body diagrams, fossils, animals)?
   - If the whole topic is abstract philosophy with nothing to draw, skip it.
4. **The Originality Check:** Are you copying an exact viral competitor title word-for-word?
   - **Rule:** Never clone a competitor's exact title or thumbnail (`@AncestorFiless` proved cloning *What Did Ancient Humans Actually Do All Day?* fails). Bring a fresh modern-to-ancient angle.

## Step-by-Step Action
1. Open `03_Topic_Ideas/Topic_Database_100_Ideas.md`.
2. Rotate across our 4 pillars so your channel never feels repetitive:
   - **Week 1:** Pillar 1 (Ancient Brain in a Modern World)
   - **Week 2:** Pillar 2 (How Ancient Humans Actually Survived)
   - **Week 3:** Pillar 3 (Weird Glitches of the Human Body)
   - **Week 4:** Pillar 4 (Origins of Everyday Habits & Inventions)
3. Mark your chosen topic `[R]` (Researching) in the database.
4. Copy `08_Video_Production/_VIDEO_TEMPLATE` into a new folder inside `08_Video_Production/` named like `EP01_3AM_Wakeup` and fill in `video_brief.md`.
