# Step 3 Guide: How to Write an Engaging YouTube Script

> **Goal:** Write a tight, conversational **12 to 15 minute script (~1,850 to 2,250 words)** that hooks viewers in 15 seconds and keeps them watching until the final callback.

---

## Why 12–15 Minutes Works Best
Our live competitor audit proved:
- **Too short (3–6 min):** Stalls under 300 views (`@AncestorFiless`) because total watch time per click is too low.
- **Too long & padded (30–38 min):** Causes viewer drop-off and repetitive AI filler (`@Neon-Rush-3D`).
- **Sweet spot (12–15 min):** Qualifies for mid-roll ads, maximizes Browse/Suggested push, and keeps pacing crisp.

---

## The Proven 4-Part Story Blueprint

### Part 1: The 45-Second Modern-to-Ancient Hook (0:00–0:45)
- **Seconds 0–15 (Drop straight into a relatable second-person moment):** No logo intro, no *"Welcome back to the channel!"*
  - *Example:* *"It's 3:07 AM. Your house is completely silent. Nobody called you. Nothing bumped the window. Yet your eyes snap wide open—and your heart is already racing."*
- **Seconds 15–30 (State the conventional myth & flip it):**
  - *Example:* *"Modern advice tells you your brain is broken—that you have stress insomnia. Wait a second, though. Because if waking up at 3 AM is a defect, why do healthy humans across almost every culture on Earth do the exact same thing?"*
- **Seconds 30–45 (Promise the evidence & open the main mystery loop):**
  - *Example:* *"To find out, historians had to read 500-year-old diaries—and anthropologists had to strap sleep trackers onto modern hunter-gatherers. What they found flips everything you were told about an eight-hour night."*

### Part 2: 6 to 7 Escalating Chapters (0:45–11:30)
- Give every chapter ONE clear question and answer it using simple everyday comparisons (ELI5 analogies).
- **Plant 3 "Remember X" breadcrumbs** early and pay them off later (*"Remember that one-hour quiet window—it becomes crucial in a moment"*).
- **Add 1 Viewer Question / Quiz moment** around minute 5–6 (to pair with YouTube's interactive Quiz card, just like `@Inkexplainer96`'s 19M-view video).
- **Use short conversational sentences** (10–18 words average). Never write academic essays meant for reading silently.

### Part 3: The Modern Payoff & Reframe (11:30–12:45)
- Bring the ancient story right back to the viewer's modern Tuesday afternoon so they walk away feeling genuinely smarter—not just entertained.

### Part 4: Full-Circle Callback + One-Video Bridge (12:45–13:30)
- Echo your opening 3:07 AM image, skip long "smash like and subscribe" begging, and point to **one** related video on your channel.

## Mandatory Human Script Review (Before Voiceover!)
1. Copy `04_Scripts/Prompt_02_YouTube_Scriptwriting.md` to generate the draft, then run `04_Scripts/Prompt_03_Script_Fact_Checking.md`.
2. **Read the entire script aloud once.**
3. **Delete AI giveaways:** Cut phrases like *"In today's fast-paced world"*, *"delve into"*, *"tapestry"*, *"it's important to note"*, or prompt notes like *"pattern interrupt"*.
4. Save the final approved text as `01_script/script_APPROVED.md`.
