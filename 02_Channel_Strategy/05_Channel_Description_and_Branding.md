## 3. Visual Branding Direction (Avatar, Banner & Typography)

### Profile Avatar (`800 × 800 px` Circle-Safe)
- **Design Concept:** Clean circular editorial illustration on a warm cream paper backdrop (`#F6F1E7`) with a thin charcoal ink border: a minimalist split-head silhouette or expressive side-profile of our editorial protagonist—left half subtly illuminated by warm campfire amber (`#C85A32`), right half subtly lit by cool midnight blue clock/screen glow (`#25344A`).
- **Rule:** Zero tiny text inside the avatar (it must read instantly at 32×32 px on mobile comments).

### Channel Banner (`2560 × 1440 px`, Safe Text Area `1546 × 423 px` Centered)
- **Background:** Warm textured cream paper (`#F6F1E7`) framed gently by light charcoal editorial doodles in the corners (a glowing alarm clock, a bone flute, a campfire, a human brain sketch, an obsidian spear, a coffee cup).
- **Center Typography:**
  - Bold Charcoal Serif All-Caps: **HUMAN MISMATCH**
  - Clean Terracotta Sans-Serif Below: `ANCIENT HARDWARE • MODERN WORLD`
  - Small subtle tagline: `New illustrated deep-dives every week`

### Channel Color Palette & Fonts (Use Everywhere: Thumbnails, Diagrams, Banner)
- **Warm Cream Paper:** `#F6F1E7` *(Primary light canvas)*
- **Deep Charcoal Ink:** `#1E2228` *(Outlines, dark text, authority)*
- **Terracotta Rust:** `#C85A32` *(Primary signature accent)*
- **Forest Sage:** `#4E6E58` *(Nature, anthropology, science highlights)*
- **Ochre Gold:** `#D9A441` *(Warm firelight, attention highlights)*
- **Midnight Slate:** `#25344A` *(Night scenes, high-contrast thumbnail backgrounds)*
- **Fonts (100% Free Commercial Google Fonts — available in Canva & Photopea):**
  - *Headers / Thumbnails:* **Montserrat ExtraBold** or **Oswald Bold** (clean, punchy mobile legibility)
  - *Editorial Accents / Dates / Chapter Cards:* **Playfair Display Bold** or **Merriweather Bold**
