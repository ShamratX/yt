# Complete Channel Category Structure & Content Pillars

To keep the channel focused for YouTube's recommendation algorithm while supporting **500+ long-term videos without repetition**, every video must fit into **one of 4 Core Content Pillars**.

---

## Pillar 1: Ancient Brain in a Modern World (40% of Uploads)
*Core Theme: Why your modern psychology, emotions, and daily mental frustrations are actually ancient survival mechanisms running in the wrong century.*

### Subcategories
- **1A. Sleep, Dreams & Nighttime Brain Glitches:** 3 AM wakeups, nightmares, sleep paralysis, hypnic jerks (falling twitches), teenager late-night clocks, why we forget dreams.
- **1B. Social Anxiety, Status & Tribal Wiring:** Replaying old embarrassing memories, fear of public speaking, imposter syndrome, gossip as social glue, fear of rejection, loneliness in big cities.
- **1C. Attention, Cravings & Modern Mismatches:** Doomscrolling & foraging loops, procrastination (temporal discounting), boredom pain, sugar/salt/fat cravings, work burnout & alarm clocks.
- **1D. Memory, Perception & Weird Mind Quirks:** Inner monologue vs. silence, nostalgia, why time speeds up as you age, déjà vu, cognitive biases rooted in survival, heartbreak & social pain.

### What Videos Belong Here
Videos starting with a **relatable modern feeling** (*"Why does your brain..."*) and proving through anthropology/neuroscience that it once kept humans alive.

---

## Pillar 2: How Ancient Humans Actually Survived (25% of Uploads)
*Core Theme: What ordinary daily life—not kings or battles—really felt like before modern medicine, tech, and convenience.*

### Subcategories
- **2A. Ancient Health, Pain & Medicine:** Toothaches without dentists, healing broken bones, childbirth before hospitals, surviving infections/plagues, caring for elders, primitive surgery & herbal remedies.
- **2B. Extreme Weather, Shelter & Daily Logistics:** Surviving -40°C Ice Age winters, monsoon/rainy weeks, keeping fire alive, sleeping before mattresses, finding clean drinking water, preserving food without fridges.
- **2C. Prehistoric Travel, Navigation & Trade:** Crossing open oceans without compasses, 1,000-mile Stone Age trade networks, navigating deserts and tundras, migrating out of Africa.
- **2D. Childhood, Play, Art & Social Life:** How ancient kids learned without schools, ancient parties & feasting, deep cave art ("prehistoric cinema"), stone-age music, ancient humor & games.

### What Videos Belong Here
Immersive, evidence-backed storytelling episodes (`How Ancient Humans Survived...` / `What [Everyday Event] Actually Looked Like 20,000 Years Ago`) grounded in archaeology and ethnography.

---

## Pillar 3: Weird Glitches of the Human Body (20% of Uploads)
*Core Theme: Surprising biological design trade-offs and anatomical mysteries everyone notices on their own body.*

### Subcategories
- **3A. Uniquely Human Traits:** Emotional tears, blushing, chins, white sclera (eyes), accurate throwing shoulders, menopause (Grandmother Hypothesis), hairlessness & sweat cooling.
- **3B. Evolutionary Trade-Offs & Body Flaws:** Lower back pain from walking upright, wisdom teeth & shrinking jaws, helpless newborns (Obstetrical Dilemma), motion sickness, blind spots, aging limits.
- **3C. Hidden Defenses & Reflexes:** Goosebumps without fur, hiccups, yawning contagion, disgust reflex (behavioral immune system), blood type plague wars, fingerprints, shivering & fever.
- **3D. Human Diversity & Adaptation:** High-altitude blood adaptations, lactose tolerance evolution, diving reflexes (Bajau sea nomads), handedness (90% right-handed), circadian chronotypes.

### What Videos Belong Here
Punchy curiosity hooks about everyday physical traits (`Why Humans Are the Only Animals That...` / `The Real Reason Your Body...`) explained with clear visual anatomy/evolution diagrams.

---

## Pillar 4: Origins of Everyday Habits & Human Inventions (15% of Uploads)
*Core Theme: How ordinary habits, foods, animals, and simple inventions quietly rewired human biology and civilization.*

### Subcategories
- **4A. Biology-Changing Breakthroughs:** Cooking food & brain growth, wearing stitched clothes, domesticating wolves/dogs, taming horses, fermenting drinks, brewing coffee/tea.
- **4B. Everyday Comforts & Hygiene:** Origins of soap & bathing myths, indoor plumbing, beds & pillows, eyeglasses, dental brushes, anesthesia, footwear & running mechanics.
- **4C. Measuring & Organizing Human Life:** Telling time before clocks, how mechanical clocks invented "being late", calendars & missing days, time zones, money & gift economies, writing & paper.
- **4D. Invisible Materials That Shaped Daily Life:** Salt, clear glass, refrigeration, spices, spices/sugar/bread domestication, pottery & food storage.

### What Videos Belong Here
Story-driven origin documentaries connecting **one everyday object or habit** directly back to how it changed human bodies, minds, or daily routines.

---

## Recommended Upload Rotation (Every 4 Weeks)
To prevent audience fatigue and keep Suggested-video links strong, rotate across pillars naturally:
- **Video 1:** Pillar 1 (Modern Brain Mismatch)
- **Video 2:** Pillar 2 (Ancient Survival / Daily Life)
- **Video 3:** Pillar 3 (Weird Human Body Glitch)
- **Video 4:** Pillar 1 or Pillar 4 (Brain Mismatch / Everyday Habit Origin)
