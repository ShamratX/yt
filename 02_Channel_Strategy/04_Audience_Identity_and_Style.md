# Target Audience, Channel Identity, Storytelling & Visual Style

> **Channel Name:** **Human Mismatch** (`@HumanMismatch`)  
> **Primary Niche:** Human Origins, Evolutionary Paradoxes & Everyday Anthropology

---

## 1. Target Audience
- **Age & Gender:** Primarily **18 to 45** (healthy male/female balance, skewing slightly 55% male / 45% female across science/history/psychology viewers).
- **Geography:** Global English-speaking Tier-1 core (**United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Western Europe**) plus high-engagement international English viewers (**India, Singapore, Philippines, Eastern Europe**)—expanding further once YouTube Auto-Dubbing is enabled.
- **Viewer Psychology:**
  - Curious everyday thinkers who enjoy *Ink Explainer*, *Veritasium*, *Kurzgesagt*, *TED-Ed*, and *Basically Primitive*.
  - Often watch during evening wind-down, meals, commuting, or late-night rabbit holes.
  - Love clicking questions that explain *their own weird habits, fears, sleep, or body*—and love sharing a mind-blowing 1-sentence takeaway with friends the next day.

---

## 2. Channel Identity & Personality
- **Persona:** The curious, grounded science storyteller—smart, empathetic, slightly dry-humored, **never** preachy, clickbaity, or robotic.
- **Core Promise:** *"You aren't broken—you're just running 300,000-year-old human hardware in the wrong century."*
- **Credibility Pillar:** Every episode cites real published anthropology, biology, and psychology studies in the description, and honestly notes where scientists still debate.

---

## 3. Storytelling Style
1. **Modern-to-Ancient Bridge:** Always start in the viewer's modern life (first 15 seconds), reveal a surprising Darwinian/historical paradox (seconds 15–45), rewind into vivid ancient or experimental proof (minutes 1–11), and return to modern life with a crisp reframing payoff (final 2 minutes).
2. **Zero Academic Dryness:** Translate every technical concept into a simple everyday comparison (ELI5 clarity inspired by `@Crayon_Capital`).
3. **Retention Devices:**
   - **3 "Remember X" breadcrumbs** planted early and paid off chapters later (inspired by `@HistoryInMinsYT`).
   - **1 interactive YouTube Quiz** mid-video (inspired by `@Inkexplainer96`'s 19M hit).
   - **Short conversational sentences** (10–18 words average) spoken at a calm, steady documentary pace.
   - **Full-circle callback outro** with no desperate "smash like and subscribe" begging.

---

## 4. Visual Style (Standing Out From "AI Stickman Fatigue")
- **Locked Aesthetic:** Warm **editorial pen-and-ink + soft watercolor/gouache 2D illustrations** on subtle textured cream parchment (`#F6F1E7`) for daytime/science scenes and muted midnight slate (`#25344A`) with warm amber firelight (`#E59A3C`) for night/prehistoric scenes.
- **Character Anchors:** Recurring simplified 2D editorial protagonist (muted terracotta sweater in modern scenes; practical earth-toned tunic in prehistoric scenes)—NOT plain white-circle stickmen.
- **Visual Variety:** Rotate 55–75 original scenes per episode across wide landscapes, expressive close-ups, split-screen Modern-vs-Ancient frames, overhead maps, and 6–8 crisp Canva data/comparison diagrams.
- **Zero Burned-In Clutter on Long-Form:** Keep artwork clean on screen; provide accurate `.srt` captions via YouTube CC.

---

## 5. Recommended Video Length & Format Split
- **Long-Form Sweet Spot:** **12 to 16 minutes** (~1,850 to 2,350 spoken words). Long enough for mid-roll ads and strong Browse/Suggested watch time; tight enough to hold 45–55%+ Average Percentage Viewed without filler.
- **Format Split:** **90% Long-Form** (core channel growth + high AdSense RPM) + optional **10% Companion Shorts** (starting Month 2: one 40-second myth-busting highlight per week linked directly to the full episode via YouTube's *Related Video* feature).
