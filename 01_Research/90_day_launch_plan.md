## 2. Target Audience & Content Pillars

### Target Audience Profile
- **Core Demographic:** Global English-speaking adults and older teens (ages 18–45; broad male/female balance, strongest Tier-1 reach in US, UK, Canada, Australia, Western Europe, plus large English-fluent audiences in India/SE Asia).
- **Psychographics:** Curious minds who love *Kurzgesagt*, *Veritasium*, *Ink Explainer*, *Ted-Ed*, and *Basically Primitive*; often watch while winding down, commuting, or procrastinating; love sharing "did you know why we actually do that?" facts with friends.

4. **Pillar 4 — Ancient Origins of Modern Habits & Culture (15%):** Gossip, music, cooking, sugar cravings, dogs, timekeeping, trading networks. *(Endless topic elasticity + natural bridge to invention stories)*

## 3. Realistic 90-Day Upload Schedule & Production Rhythm

> **Pace Principle:** Never publish 5 rushed videos/week like `@Neon-Rush-3D` (whose recent filler uploads stalled at 2K–9K views). Match `@Inkexplainer96`'s quality-first discipline early, then scale cleanly once your templates are dialed in.

- **Days 1–30 (Month 1 — Foundation & System Calibration):** **1 Long-Form Video/Week** (4 videos) + **0 Shorts** *(focus 100% on dialing in your script, art style, voice, and thumbnail system)*.
- **Days 31–60 (Month 2 — Momentum & Companion Shorts Test):** **2 Long-Form Videos/Week** (or 3 every 2 weeks = 6–8 videos) + **1 Companion Short/Week** clipped from your best long-form 40-second myth-busting moment (linked via YouTube's "Related Video" button).
- **Days 61–90 (Month 3 — Optimization & Doubling Down):** **2 Long-Form Videos/Week** (8–10 videos; **18–22 total long-form uploads in 90 days**, with Topics #23–#30 queued for Month 4 so your pipeline never runs dry!).
- **Recommended Weekly Solo Time Budget (~10–12 hrs per long-form video):**
  - **Day 1 (2 hrs):** Pick topic, gather 3–5 real studies, draft & human-edit 2,000-word script + hook.
  - **Day 2 (1.5 hrs):** Generate & proof-listen voiceover; break script into 55–65 scene prompts.
  - **Day 3–4 (3 hrs):** Batch-generate 55–65 editorial 2D scenes (Leonardo.ai) + 6 Canva diagrams.
  - **Day 5 (3 hrs):** Assemble timeline in Clipchamp / DaVinci Resolve, add slow Ken Burns motion, YouTube Audio Library music/SFX, export `.srt` captions.
  - **Day 6 (1.5 hrs):** Design 2 thumbnail variants (for YouTube's built-in Test & Compare), write SEO title/description/citations, add 1 YouTube Quiz card, publish.

## 5. Packaging, Retention & Format Strategy

### Suggested Video Lengths
- **Sweet Spot: 12 to 16 minutes** (~1,800–2,400 words).
- **Evidence Backing This:** `@AncestorFiless`'s 3–6 min videos stalled under 266 views; `@Inkexplainer96`'s breakouts are 11–21 min; `@HistoryInMinsYT` & `@Crayon_Capital` thrive at 14–18 min. At 12–16 min, you qualify for mid-roll ads (8+ min), build strong session watch time, and avoid `@Neon-Rush-3D`'s 35-minute padding trap.

### Shorts vs. Long-Form Strategy
- **90% Long-Form / 10% Companion Shorts (starting Month 2).**
- Never create standalone teen-hack Shorts (`@unlockthemindyt` trap). Every Short must be a **35–45 second self-contained curiosity teaser** from a published long-form episode (e.g., *"Why hunter-gatherers actually had fewer cavities than modern humans..."*) with the YouTube **"Related Video"** link pointing directly to the 14-minute episode.
- If a Short does not drive meaningful clicks to your long-form catalog after 4 weeks of testing, pause Shorts and reallocate that time to improving long-form thumbnails and hooks.

### Thumbnail Strategy (Anti-Stickman-Fatigue System)
1. **Signature Look:** Warm textured paper/parchment backdrop + **editorial ink-and-watercolor character** (distinctive recurring protagonist—e.g., slightly rumpled modern human in a muted teal/mustard sweater contrasted against an ancient counterpart).
2. **Split-Contrast Visual Hook:** Show the *modern tension vs. ancient cause* visually in 1 glance (e.g., exhausted person staring at a glowing `3:00 AM` alarm clock on the left vs. glowing predator eyes outside a campfire on the right).
3. **Text Rule:** **0 to 3 words MAX**, large clean bold sans-serif or hand-lettered serif in high-contrast cream/yellow/red with subtle dark shadow—never repeat the full title! (e.g., Title: *Why Your Brain Wakes You Up at 3 AM* → Thumbnail text: `3:00 AM` or `NOT INSOMNIA`).
4. **Mandatory A/B Testing:** Use YouTube Studio's built-in **Test & Compare** on *every single upload* with 2 genuinely different concepts (Concept A: Character emotion close-up; Concept B: Split-screen Modern vs. Ancient contrast).

### Title Strategy (4 Proven High-CTR Templates)
1. **The Modern-Glitch Twist:** `Why Your Brain [Relatable Annoyance] (It Saved Your Ancestors)`
2. **The Evolutionary Paradox:** `Why Evolution Never Deleted [Seemingly Bad Trait]`
3. **The Contrarian Flip:** `Why [Underrated Habit] Built Human Civilization (Not [Obvious Thing])`
4. **The Survival Curiosity Gap:** `How Ancient Humans Survived [Visceral Problem] Without [Modern Tool]`
- Keep titles **50–65 characters** so mobile screens never cut off the punchline.

### Audience Retention Strategy (Minute-by-Minute Blueprint)
- **0:00–0:15 (Pattern-Interrupt Modern Hook):** Start directly in second-person sensory reality (*"It's 3:07 AM. The room is silent. You aren't thirsty, there's no noise—yet your eyes snap wide open..."*). Zero channel intro logos, zero *"Hey guys welcome back!"*
- **0:15–0:45 (The Paradox + Proof Promise):** State the conventional assumption, flip it (*"Modern doctors call it insomnia—but anthropologists studying hunter-gatherers discovered something far stranger..."*), and open 1 clear mystery loop.
- **0:45–11:30 (6–7 Tight Chapters + Open Loops):**
  - Cut visuals every **5–8 seconds** in Minute 1, then every **8–12 seconds** thereafter.
  - Plant **3 explicit breadcrumbs** (*"Remember that hormone spike—it's going to explain everything in minute 8"* — proven on `@HistoryInMinsYT`).
  - Insert **1 YouTube Quiz** around minute 5–6 (proven on `@Inkexplainer96`'s 19M hit!) and 1 crisp Canva comparison chart/timeline every 3 minutes to reset viewer eyes.
  - Cross-reference your own earlier uploads naturally (*"Just like we saw in our 3 AM sleep video..."*) to trigger binge sessions.
- **11:30–13:30 (Full-Circle Callback + Next-Video Bridge):** Resolve the opening 3 AM scene poetically, **skip generic "smash like and subscribe" begging**, and point directly to ONE related end-screen video (*"If your brain wakes you up at 3 AM, wait until you see why it replays 10-year-old embarrassing memories right before you fall asleep—watch this next."*).

## 6. Analytics to Monitor & Post-10-Uploads Improvement Protocol

### The 5 Core YouTube Studio Metrics That Actually Matter
| Metric | Healthy Early Benchmark (New Channel Est.) | What It Diagnoses |
|---|---|---|
| **Impressions CTR (Browse/Home)** | **5.5% – 8.5%+** *(4%–5% = weak packaging)* | Whether your **Topic + Title + Thumbnail** beats competitors on the home feed |
| **30-Second Retention** | **68% – 78%+** *(below 60% = broken hook)* | Whether your opening 30 seconds matched the thumbnail promise and hooked immediately |
| **Average Percentage Viewed (APV)** | **42% – 55%+** on 12–15 min videos | Overall script pacing, chapter transitions, and visual freshness |
| **Returning Viewers & End-Screen Clicks** | Rising steadily after Video #5; **3%+** end-screen CTR | Whether viewers trust your channel brand enough to binge multiple videos |
| **Traffic Source Mix** | Growing **Browse Features & Suggested** share | Confirmation that YouTube's algorithm understands who your target viewer is |

### The Post-First-10-Uploads Diagnostic & Iteration Protocol
Do **not** panic or change niches during Uploads #1–#10—treat your first 10 videos as your paid market-research dataset. After Video #10 has been live for 7 days, run this exact audit:

1. **Rank All 10 Videos by `Views in First 7 Days` and `CTR × APV`:**
   - Identify your **Top 2 Winners** (highest impressions + highest CTR/APV) and **Bottom 2 Losers**.
2. **Diagnose Using the 2×2 Matrix:**
   - **High CTR (>7%) + Low Retention (<38%):** Great topic/thumbnail, weak script pacing or over-promising hook → tighten intro to <35s, cut academic hedging, add faster visual resets.
   - **Low CTR (<4.5%) + High Retention (>50%):** Great video trapped in bad packaging → immediately redesign thumbnail & rewrite title on Videos #1–#10 (remember `@Inkexplainer96` and `@HistoryInMinsYT` both saw **+280% to +365% VPH surges** after post-upload thumbnail swaps!).
   - **High CTR + High Retention:** **You found a winning sub-pillar!** Immediately schedule 3–4 follow-up episodes exploring adjacent angles of that exact pillar for Month 3.
   - **Low CTR + Low Retention:** Retire that sub-angle; don't force it.
3. **Audit Comment Sentiment:** Read every comment on your top 3 videos—viewers will literally hand you your next 20 viral video titles (*"Can you do one on why we forget dreams?"*, *"Wait, what about left-handedness?"*).
4. **Upgrade One Bottleneck at Month 2–3:**
   - If audio feedback mentions robotic voice → invest \$6–\$22/mo in **ElevenLabs Starter/Creator**.
   - If visual feedback mentions repetition → move assembly from Clipchamp to **DaVinci Resolve Free** for smoother camera motion and layered sound design.
   - Enable **YouTube Auto-Dubbing** (once eligible in YouTube Studio) and keep scripts free of untranslatable slang to capture global views automatically.
