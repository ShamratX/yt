## 5 Strategic Validations From These 5 Mega-Channels (87.2M Combined Subs)

1. **`Education` Is the Billion-View Umbrella:** All 5 mega-channels sit under `Education` — covering Science, Human Biology, Ancient Civilization, Everyday Psychology, Everyday Object History, and World History.
2. **Long-Form 2D Beats Shorts at Mega-Scale:** OverSimplified has **0 Shorts**; TED-Ed, Infographics Show, and Psych2Go have all slowed Shorts sharply over the last 6–12 months; only Kurzgesagt runs active Shorts alongside 15-min long-form videos.
3. **Minimal, Clean 2D Art Scales to 13M–25M+ Subscribers:** Psych2Go (13.2M), TED-Ed (23M), OverSimplified (9.7M), and Kurzgesagt (25.8M) all prove **uncluttered, minimal 2D design with clean negative space** wins over busy, messy visuals.
4. **YouTube `Auto-Dubbed` Is Standard Among Mega-Channels:** 3 of the 5 (`Infographics Show`, `Psych2Go`, `OverSimplified`) use YouTube Auto-Dubbing, and `Kurzgesagt` operates 6 separate localized channels.
5. **Our Winning Niche (`Human Mismatch`) Sits Directly in the Sweet Spot of the Top 4 Giants:** It merges the highest-demand, safest evergreen pillars of **Kurzgesagt** (biology/civilization/brain), **TED-Ed** (human body + everyday food/habit history), and **Psych2Go** (relatable everyday psychology) — without fighting OverSimplified in saturated WW2/military history.
